Current against voltage

What to notice. Double the voltage and the current doubles. Double the resistance and the current halves. The dots move faster when the current is larger.

With the LED, the LED takes about 2.0 V and the resistor takes the rest. Below 2.0 V, almost no current flows.

This page uses a simple LED model: 0 A below 2.0 V, exactly 2.0 V above it. A real LED changes a little with current (1.8–2.1 V).
